💰 Scoring & Coins

Points: Cleared lines and hard drops award points. Combos and levels multiply your score multiplier.

Tetra-Coins: Earn coins by clearing lines (5 coins per line), reaching score milestones (25 coins per 1,000 points), or winning multiplayer matches (100 coins).

🗑️ Garbage Blocks (Multiplayer)

When you clear 2+ lines in battle mode, send garbage rows to your opponent. Garbage rows have one open hole. Your opponent can cancel garbage by clearing lines.

Tip: Build combos to send more garbage and pressure your opponent!

🎯 Game Modes

Endless: Survive progressive speed increases and chase a local high score.

Split-Screen Battle: First player to 10,000 points wins. Combat system uses garbage blocks and line clears for attacks.

Daily Challenge: A unique modifier changes every day. Compete for the best score with today's challenge!

Daily Run: The date is the seed, so everyone gets the same seven-bag piece sequence.

Special blocks: Bombs blast a 3×3 area on line clear, drills clear their column below, and lasers erase their landing row.

⭐ Tips & Tricks

• Press M to mute/unmute audio
• Press P to pause your game
• Hold a piece strategically to set up combos
• Watch for garbage—cancel it early with multi-line clears
• Higher levels = faster drops and higher scoring multipliers
